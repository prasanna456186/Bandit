# Level 9 → Level 10

`data.txt` is mostly binary garbage this time, but somewhere in there is a human-readable string preceded by a few `=` characters.

## What I did

`strings` pulls out printable text from a binary file, which is exactly what I needed here:

```bash
strings data.txt | grep '='
```

`strings` gives all the readable chunks, then piping that through `grep '='` filters it down to just the lines that have an equals sign, since the level says the password comes after some `=`.
