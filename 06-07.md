# Level 6 → Level 7

This time the file isn't even in our home directory — it's "somewhere on the server", owned by bandit7, group bandit6, and 33 bytes.

## What I did

Since we don't know the location at all, `find` has to search from root:

```bash
find / -user bandit7 -group bandit6 -size 33c 2>/dev/null
```

The `2>/dev/null` part is important — without it you get flooded with "Permission denied" errors from folders we can't read, which makes the real result hard to spot. Redirecting stderr to /dev/null just throws those away and leaves the actual match(es) on screen.
