# Level 8 → Level 9

`data.txt` here has a ton of lines, and the level says the password is the one line that only shows up once — every other line is duplicated somewhere in the file.

## What I did

Standard sort + uniq combo for this kind of thing:

```bash
sort data.txt | uniq -u
```

`uniq` only removes duplicates that are next to each other, which is why you have to `sort` first — that groups all identical lines together. `-u` tells it to only print lines that had no duplicates at all, which leaves just the one unique line.
